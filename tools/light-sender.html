zhww light sender

Flashes UR strings at the device's light sensor. Paste one or more ready UR strings below, aim the device at the screen, and press Start. Each line is sent as one frame, the whole sequence repeats until you stop. The device answers with a QR code; this page only sends.

The page is limited by the screen's refresh rate: a display can only change what it shows once per refresh, so it cannot flash faster than that. These rates are exact divisors of a 60 Hz refresh (1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 frames per symbol). The page measures the real refresh rate when you press Start and reports the effective rate: on a 120 Hz panel the same choices are still exact (2, 4, 6, 8 and 12 frames per symbol), while on a panel whose rate is not a multiple of 60 Hz (90 Hz, say) the effective rate has to differ from the label, because a symbol can only start on a frame boundary.

Format, timing and caveats

preamble 0xAA 0xAA | version(1) | length(2, LE) | payload | CRC-32(4, LE)

  • CRC-32 is IEEE (reflected 0xEDB88320, init 0xFFFFFFFF, inverted at the end) over version | length | payload - the preamble is excluded.
  • Manchester, IEEE 802.3: bit 1 is dark then light, bit 0 is light then dark. MSB first, two symbols per bit. The opposite convention is the exact inverse and only the CRC catches it.
  • The frame stream is continuous: frames follow each other with no gap, and the last frame wraps straight into the first one of the next pass.
  • Symbol timing comes from requestAnimationFrame, so it follows the display refresh instead of drifting the way a timer would. If the browser stalls or the tab is hidden, the stream pauses rather than skipping symbols - a gap the device can resynchronise from, not corrupted data. A single dropped frame does stretch one symbol; the CRC rejects that frame and the next pass retries it, which is what the repetition is for.
  • The device's clock recovery only pulls in over about ±30% of its configured nominal symbol rate (rate_tolerance_percent in light_rx.zig), so a rate near the firmware's nominal works best - the documented operating point is 30-60 symbols/s, which is why 30 is the default here. Slower rates are also more forgiving of a dropped frame and of mains flicker.
  • Dim the room, use full brightness, and aim the device so the sensor sees the flashing area and as little of the readout overlay as possible (tap "Hide readout" if needed).
  • This page does not encode UR, does not chunk or split parts, does not read the device's QR answer, and makes no sound. It sends the exact bytes of each line.
