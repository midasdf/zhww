# The zhww name

- **Last updated**: 2026-10-14

The code, the hardware design and the documents are open source under the licences in
[LICENSE-POLICY.md](LICENSE-POLICY.md). Those licences cover copyright; **they do not give out the
project's name**. "zhww" is not a registered trademark. This page is the project's naming policy, so
that someone who receives a device called zhww can tell what they have.

## You may, without asking

- Say what your work is based on: "a fork of zhww", "based on zhww", "compatible with zhww".
- Build, use, give away or sell devices made from the **unmodified** published design that run an
  **unmodified** published firmware release, and call them zhww.
- Write about the project, review it, teach with it, and use the name in links and titles.

## Please do not

- Distribute **modified** firmware, or devices built from a **modified** design, **under the name
  zhww** or a confusingly similar one. Give your variant its own name ("Foo, based on zhww").
  A hardware wallet's name is a promise about what it does when it signs; someone else's changes
  should not borrow that promise.
- Suggest that the project endorses, audits or supports your product.

## The logo

There is no logo yet. When one is added it will be CC-BY-SA-4.0 as artwork, and the same naming rules
apply to using it as a badge for a product.

Questions: open an issue.
